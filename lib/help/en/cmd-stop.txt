# stop · stop a service

It stops a service without touching the others and without removing it. `./deltactl deploy` starts it again.

## Usage
$ ./deltactl stop <instance> <service>

## Example
$ ./deltactl stop colorado s1

> To stop all the services of the instance use `./deltactl down <instance>`.
> `./deltactl status` will show the service as `stopped`.

## Related
- `./deltactl help services`, `./deltactl down --help`

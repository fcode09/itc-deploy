# logs · view the logs

It shows the last 200 lines of the instance's services.

## Usage
$ ./deltactl logs <instance> [service] [-f]

## Examples
$ ./deltactl logs colorado s1        # logs of s1
$ ./deltactl logs colorado s0 -f     # follow live (Ctrl+C to exit)

> `./deltactl status <instance>` counts how many lines with ERROR there were in the last 10 minutes.
> The logs rotate by themselves: 5 files of 10 MB per service.

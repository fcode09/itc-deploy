# down · stop and remove the containers

It stops and removes the containers of the instance. The data (`data/`), the secrets and the
configuration are kept: a later `./deltactl deploy` starts it again.

## Usage
$ ./deltactl down <instance> [--yes]

## What it does NOT do
- it does not delete `data/`, `secrets/` or the `.env.*` files
- it does not delete the Mongo databases
- it does not delete images

## Related
- `./deltactl stop` to stop a single service

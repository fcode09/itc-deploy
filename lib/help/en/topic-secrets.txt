# Secrets and Firebase files

## The Firebase JSON files (FCM)
`s1` needs two service account files for push notifications:
- `FCM_CERT` and `KEEPER_FCM_CERT` in `.env.s1` hold the PATH of the file, not its content

## Where they are copied
$ cp fcm.json instances/colorado/secrets/fcm.json
$ cp keeper-fcm.json instances/colorado/secrets/keeper-fcm.json
and in `.env.s1`:
$ FCM_CERT=/run/secrets/fcm.json
$ KEEPER_FCM_CERT=/run/secrets/keeper-fcm.json
> `instances/colorado/secrets/` is mounted read-only on `/run/secrets/` inside the `s1` container.
! The path in `.env.s1` is the one the container sees (`/run/secrets/...`), not the one on your disk.

## Frequent errors
- a path with `/secrets/fcm.json` instead of `/run/secrets/fcm.json`: the container does not find it
- a file with a doubled name (`fcm.json.json`): check with `ls instances/colorado/secrets/`
- the file name does not match the one in the variable

## General rules
- nothing in `instances/` is versioned: it is in `.gitignore`
- `.env.*` and `secrets/` keep restricted permissions (600 and 700)
- the secrets (`JWT_SECRET`, encryption keys...) must be unique per instance
- never paste secrets in tickets, chats or logs

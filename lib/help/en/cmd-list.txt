# list · summary of instances

One line per instance: active services, ports, tags, data state and whether it is up.

## Usage
$ ./deltactl list

## Columns
- `SERVICES` those of `COMPOSE_PROFILES`
- `S0`, `S1` ports published on 127.0.0.1
- `S0_TAG`, `S1_TAG` tags configured in `.env.instance`
- `DATA` `init` (seeded), `existing` (already had data) or `unseeded`
- `STATE` `up (N containers)` or `stopped`

> To see the version that is really running, the health and the mismatches use `./deltactl status`.

## Related
